Common Questions

Piano Lessons FAQs

What age can a child start piano lessons?

Children can start piano around age 5 or 6, as long as they can recognize basic letters and sit comfortably for 30 minutes.

Do I need an acoustic piano at home?

Not necessarily! A full-size 88-key weighted digital keyboard works wonderfully for beginner and intermediate practice.

Do adult beginners take piano lessons here?

Absolutely! Many of our adult students are learning piano for the first time or returning to it after years away.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes, we love having multiple family members learning with us. Contact us to learn more about family enrollment options.
