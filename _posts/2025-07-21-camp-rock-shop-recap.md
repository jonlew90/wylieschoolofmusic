<p><strong>Camp Rock Shop – Summer Recap</strong></p>

<p>We just wrapped up Camp Rock Shop and had an awesome time with the kids who joined us!</p>

<p>Throughout the week, the students got to form bands, learn songs together, and figure out how to play as a group. We focused on things like listening to each other, locking in rhythms, and making adjustments so everyone could sound their best. And of course, we had some fun along the way too—games, laughs, and a few snack breaks never hurt.</p>

<p>One of the best parts was seeing how much each student grew in just a few days. By the end of the week, they were more confident, more connected to their instruments, and more comfortable performing with others. Some even said this was the highlight of their summer (we’ll take it!).</p>

<p>We caught some great moments on camera, so be sure to check out the pics and video clips below.</p>

<p>Already looking forward to the next one!</p>
