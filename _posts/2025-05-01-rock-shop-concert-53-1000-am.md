<p> <strong>Rock Shop Bands Live This Saturday!</strong> 🎶</p>

<p>Come cheer on our student bands <strong>Afternoon Daydream, The Blameshifters</strong>, and <strong>Echo</strong> as they take the stage this <strong>Saturday, May 3rd at 10:00 AM</strong>!</p>

<p>The performance will be held at the <strong>Event Center at the Cross Church</strong>, right in the heart of <strong>downtown Wylie</strong>. These talented young musicians have been working hard in our Rock Shop program, and this is your chance to see them in action!</p>

<p>Whether you're a music lover, a friend or family member, or just curious about what our bands are up to — all are welcome!</p>

<p> Learn more about our Rock Shop program here: <a href="https://wylieschoolofmusic.com/bands" target="_new">https://wylieschoolofmusic.com/bands</a></p>
