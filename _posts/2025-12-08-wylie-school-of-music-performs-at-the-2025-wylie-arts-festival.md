<p>We had an awesome morning out at the <strong>Wylie Arts Festival</strong> on Saturday. A group of our students — plus one of our Rock Shop bands — came out to play a mix of <strong>Christmas and holiday music</strong> right on Ballard Ave in Downtown Wylie.</p>

<p>We were there from <strong>10:00–12:00</strong>, and the whole time we had people stopping, listening, cheering, and hanging out with us. Even though it was a bit cold, the kids showed up ready to play and did a fantastic job.</p>

<p><strong>Playing Music Right in the Heart of Downtown on Ballard Ave. </strong></p>

<p>Our setup was right along Ballard, and it turned into a fun, steady flow of performers and families. Students rotated in and out so nobody had to wait around too long, but plenty of families decided to stay and watch everyone anyway.</p>

<p><strong>Rock Shop  - Christmas edition!</strong></p>

<p>One of our Rock Shop bands jumped up to play as well, bringing a ton of energy. Anytime a full student band takes the stage, people stop and pay attention, and that’s exactly what happened.</p>
