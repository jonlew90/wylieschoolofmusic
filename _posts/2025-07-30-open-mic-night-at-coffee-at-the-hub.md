<p>We had an amazing time at our recent Open Mic Night, hosted by <em>Coffee at the Hub</em> in downtown Wylie! Students of all ages took the stage to share their talents—from classic rock tunes to original songs—and the energy in the room was incredible.</p>

<p>Events like these give our students the chance to build confidence, connect with others, and experience the joy of live performance in a relaxed, supportive setting.</p>

<p>Huge thanks to <em>Coffee at the Hub</em> for partnering with us and creating such a welcoming space for our musical community. We can’t wait for the next one!</p>
