<p>This past Saturday morning, three of our Rock Shop bands—<strong>Echo</strong>, <strong>The Blame Shifters</strong>, and <strong>Afternoon Daydream</strong>—lit up the stage at the Cross Church Event Center in Downtown Wylie! Each band brought energy, musicianship, and their own unique style to the performance. From alternative grooves to classic rock covers, the students crushed it.</p>

<p>We’re proud of all the hard work these musicians put in each week at rehearsal. And for some, this was their very first time performing in a full band—what a way to kick things off!</p>

<p>Want to be part of a student band like these? Learn more about our Rock Shop program <a href="/bands">here</a>.</p>
