Have Questions?

Guitar Lessons FAQs

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No! There are no long-term contracts at Wylie School of Music. Tuition is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime with notice.

What age can a child start guitar lessons?

We generally recommend starting around age 7, when a child has the finger strength and hand size to comfortably hold a 1/2 or 3/4 size guitar.

Do I need my own guitar before starting?

Yes, having an instrument at home allows you to practice between lessons. We are happy to recommend great beginner guitars for your budget!

Do you offer free trial lessons?

Yes! We offer a free trial lesson so you can meet an instructor, try the instrument, and make sure it's a great fit before signing up.
