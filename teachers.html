Dedicated Instructors

Passionate, Experienced Educators

Our instructors are active musicians and passionate teachers dedicated to helping each student discover their sound and build confidence.

Joseph M.

PIANO • GUITAR • BASS

Lillian H.

PIANO • BEGINNING GUITAR • BEGINNING VIOLIN

Doug T.

Ian G.

John H.

Ashlyn S.

Marvin C.
