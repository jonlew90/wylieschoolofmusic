Common Questions

Drum Lessons FAQs

What age is best to start drums?

We recommend starting around age 7 or 8 so students have the reach and coordination needed for the drum kit.

Do I need a full drum set at home right away?

No! Beginners can get started with just a practice pad and a pair of drumsticks. Electronic drum kits are also great for home practice with headphones!

Can drum students join Rock Shop bands?

Yes! Drummers are in high demand in our Rock Shop band program, giving students the chance to jam with other musicians.

Are trial lessons available?

Yes, we provide a free trial lesson so you can sit at the kit and see how exciting drum lessons are.
