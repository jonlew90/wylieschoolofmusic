The Rock Shop

Join a band to Play together, grow together, and rock together.

Let's Rock

What is The Rock Shop?

Our Rock Shop program gives students of all ages the chance to:

— Experience the excitement of being in a band!

— Practice together and learn to play songs as a group.

— Build teamwork skills in a fun, supportive environment.

— Showcase all the hard work with a live performance, sharing the stage and their passion for music!

The Rock Shop: Join a Band

Monthly tuition for the Rock Shop is $115 per month for weekly 55-minute classes.

Ready to experience the excitement of making music in a band? Send us a message below and take the first step toward joining the fun! Let’s make music together!

Some of the Rock Shop bands we offer:

  • Rock/Alternative

  • Pop

  • Praise and Worship

  • Classic Rock
