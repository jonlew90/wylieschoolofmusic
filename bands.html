Real Band Experience

Step Out of the Bedroom & Onto the Stage

Practicing solo is great, but nothing compares to the energy, collaboration, and excitement of playing in a real band! Our Rock Shop Band Program groups students together by age, skill level, and musical interests to form real performing bands.

Under the guidance of our band coaches, each group selects songs, learns how to communicate on stage, masters band dynamics, and prepares for live concerts in front of friends, family, and the Wylie community.

  • Weekly rehearsals with a professional instructor
  • Guitarists, bassists, drummers, keyboardists, and vocalists
  • Learn teamwork, stage presence, and live sound basics
  • Perform at local festivals, community events, and rock concerts
