About Us

From Guitar Lessons Wylie Texas to Wylie School of Music

Before we were the Wylie School of Music, we were Guitar Lessons Wylie Texas. Steve has been teaching music in Wylie for years, working with hundreds of students to develop their skills and discover their passion for music.

As we grew through the years and welcomed fantastic instructors for piano, drums, bass, violin, and voice, it became apparent that we needed a name that more accurately reflected our full-service community music school.

Steve and his wife Courtney run the Wylie School of Music together. They have 3 boys, 1 dog, 1 cat, and 3 fish! They love being an active part of the Wylie community and supporting local families.

Our Philosophy

Personalized, Fun, and Encouraging Lessons

Music Should Be Fun

We believe the best way to stick with an instrument is to genuinely enjoy the process. We teach fundamentals through the songs and styles you actually love.

All Ages & Skill Levels

Whether you're a 7-year-old picking up guitar for the first time or an adult returning to piano, our curriculum adapts to your pace and goals.

Real Performance Stage

Through recitals, open mic nights, and our Rock Shop band concerts, students experience the joy of performing live in front of cheering friends and family.
