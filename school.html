Meet the Instructors

  • Spencer K.

  • Meredith H.

Her first experience singing in a group was in her high school Praise Band, and she quickly ventured into contemporary pop and classical styles.

  • Doug T.

    GUITAR — BASS

He is well versed in techniques such as alternate picking, economy picking, sweep picking, and crossing picking.   • Ethan H.

  • Ian G.

    BASS — GUITAR — UKULELE

FAQs

Do I have to sign a contract for Music lessons?

There are no long-term contracts to sign at the Wylie School of Music! Sign up to get started on a weekly lesson schedule that fits your calendar, and use the free trial lesson to make sure it’s a good fit for you.

Is there an age limit for lessons at the Wylie School of Music?

Music is for everyone, and we welcome all ages at the Wylie School of Music! We recommend the minimum age of 7 years old to start guitar lessons, 8 years old for drum lessons, and 6 years old for piano lessons.

Can I try a lesson before making a commitment?

What instruments are available for lessons?

We offer lessons for guitar, piano, drums, violin, and bass guitar.
